FAQ

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Intimory has no account and no server. Your data stays on your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud sync, it goes to your own private iCloud, which we cannot access. The App Store privacy label says “Data Not Collected”.

What is free?

Unlimited logging, your last 30 days, the partner list, sexual health and reminders, 3 wishes at a time, iCloud sync and Face ID lock. Older entries are never deleted: they come back with Premium.

What does Premium add?

Your full history, complete partner profiles and the map, records, trophies, advanced stats, an unlimited wish list, the shareable recap, trying to conceive mode, Apple Health, smart reminders and CSV export.

Can someone else see my data?

Lock the app with Face ID or a PIN, hide it in the app switcher, switch to the discreet notebook icon and hide names with one tap. Notifications always use neutral text.

Is it a medical app?

No. The sexual health section helps you keep track of your own screenings, and the cycle features give estimates. Intimory is not a medical device and must not be used as contraception.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings > your name > Subscriptions. You can restore your purchases in the app at any time.
