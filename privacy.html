Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

In short: the Intimory app collects no data. The App Store privacy label says “Data Not Collected”.

Intimory is an iOS app published by Dev First. It is built so that we never see your data: there is no account, no server and no tracking in the app. This policy explains what the app stores, where, and what our website does.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter (moments, partners and their profiles, practices, notes, screenings, wishes, cycle data and settings) is stored on your iPhone, inside the app’s private storage protected by iOS.

If you turn on iCloud sync (free, in Settings), this data is also stored in your own private iCloud database, linked to your Apple Account and managed by Apple. We have no access to it.

Apple features used on your device

  • Face ID, Touch ID or PIN: unlocking happens on the device. The app never receives biometric data; your PIN is stored as a hash on the device.
  • Notifications: reminders (screening, PrEP, cycle, daily reminder) are scheduled locally on the device and use neutral text.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: “Log a moment in Intimory” only opens the app. Voice requests are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.
  • Apple Health (optional, Premium): with your permission, the app writes your moments as “sexual activity” in Health. You can revoke this at any time in the Health app.
  • City search (partner profiles): the city name you type is sent to Apple Maps to find the place. We do not receive it.
  • Purchases: subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple (StoreKit). We never see your payment details.

No tracking in the app

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDK. It does not use the advertising identifier and does not share anything with third parties.

Sensitive data

Your sex life and sexual health are sensitive data. Because they stay on your device (and, if you choose, in your private iCloud), they remain under your sole control. Use the Face ID or PIN lock, the discreet icon and the option that hides names for extra privacy.

Retention and deletion

You can delete a moment, a partner, a screening or a wish at any time, or erase everything in Settings > Delete all data. This also removes the entries the app wrote to Apple Health.

Uninstalling the app deletes the data stored on the device. If iCloud sync is on, your iCloud copy is kept until you delete it in iOS Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Our website

The intimory.app website measures its audience with Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel only if you accept in the cookie banner. If you refuse or do not answer, no tracker is loaded. You can change your choice at any time with the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of each page.

With your consent, Google and Meta receive technical data about your visit (pages viewed, device, approximate location, cookie identifiers) under their own policies. No data from the app is ever sent to the website.

Age

Intimory is intended for adults. It is rated 16+ on the App Store; do not use it if you are under the legal age in your country.

Your rights

As we hold no data from the app, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete: you control it directly in the app. For the website, you can withdraw your consent at any time. You may also contact us, or lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact@intimory.app.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
